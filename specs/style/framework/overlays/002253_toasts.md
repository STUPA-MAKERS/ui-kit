# A toast is sent through the toast service

An app must show a message only through `ToastService.show({ text, kind?: 'info' | 'success' |
'warning' | 'error', action?: Action, sticky?: boolean, closed?, icon?, iconPosition?, placement?,
closable? })`. A toast with only a text and a kind may use the short form `show(text, kind?)`,
which must do the same as `show({ text, kind })`. The service must have no other way in: no
`success`, `error`, `showAction` or `showNotice`. A notice that the user cannot close (offline, update) must use `sticky: true` and
`closable: false`. The kit must place a toast at the top on a phone and at the
bottom end on a wide screen (000562), close it by a swipe, its close button or its time (000564),
and announce it (000517). An error of a request must show as a toast (000401).

Reason: One toast service keeps every message in the same place.

Test: Unit tests of `ToastService` and `app-toast`.
