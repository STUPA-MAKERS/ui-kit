# A toast is sent through the toast service

An app must show a message only through `ToastService.show({ text, kind: 'info' | 'error',
action?: Action, sticky?: boolean })`. The kit must place a toast at the top on a phone and at the
bottom end on a wide screen (000562), close it by a swipe, its close button or its time (000564),
and announce it (000517). An error of a request must show as a toast (000401).

Reason: One toast service keeps every message in the same place.

Test: Unit tests of `ToastService` and `app-toast`.
